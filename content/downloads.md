## Other resources

- [Browse the file server](https://downloads.openwrt.org/) — full archive of every release, every architecture.
- [Snapshot builds](https://downloads.openwrt.org/snapshots/) — automatic builds of `main`. Use only if you know what you are doing.
- [Source archives](https://sources.openwrt.org/) — buildable sources for every release.
- [Mirrors](https://openwrt.org/downloads) — community-hosted mirrors of the main archive.
- [Verifying signatures](https://openwrt.org/docs/guide-user/security/signatures) — check that what you downloaded matches what we shipped.
