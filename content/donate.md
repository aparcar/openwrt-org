## Monetary donations

The OpenWrt project relies on volunteers globally and has recurring monthly infrastructure expenses. Those wishing to support the initiative can contribute financially.

The [Software Freedom Conservancy](https://sfconservancy.org/) manages all collected funds (minus transaction and maintenance fees) on behalf of the OpenWrt project, directing resources toward:

- Server infrastructure
- Trademark legal management
- Travel support for developer meetings

## Hardware donations

Interested parties can propose hardware contributions by contacting developers through the [OpenWrt forum](https://forum.openwrt.org/) or via the [OpenWrt developer mailing list](/contact/).
