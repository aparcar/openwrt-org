The OpenWrt Summit 2024 takes place on **18–19 May 2024** in Cyprus, co-located with **Battlemesh v16** (15–20 May).

- **18 May** — Tatlısu
- **19 May** — Nicosia

This year's summit doubles as OpenWrt's **20th-anniversary gathering**. The format is deliberately relaxed: technical sessions during the day, social activities and an auction of an OpenWrt One in the evenings, plus an anniversary cake.

Attendees are encouraged to arrive by 14 May and stay through 20 May to take in the full Battlemesh schedule. The event is free and open to anyone interested in OpenWrt, mesh networking, or wireless community infrastructure.

Details and travel notes are in the announcement on `openwrt-adm`.
