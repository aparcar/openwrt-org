OpenWrt is shipped as a series of stable release lines. The most recent two series receive security updates; older lines are end-of-life and listed here for historical reference. Pick a series for its release history; the per-version download links live on [Downloads](/downloads/).

For the project's release-support policy and the current support status of each series, see [Security](/security/).
