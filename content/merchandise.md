OpenWrt is a non-profit project and does not produce or sell merchandise directly. Independent vendors offer OpenWrt-themed apparel and accessories; some donate a portion of revenue back to the project.

If you run a business selling OpenWrt-branded items and would like to be listed here, please [get in touch](/contact/). All use of the OpenWrt name and logo on merchandise must comply with the [trademark policy](/trademark/).

## Vendors

### FreeWear

- **Site:** [freewear.org/OpenWrt](https://www.freewear.org/OpenWrt)
- **Products:** T-shirts, hoodies
- **Ships from:** Spain
- **International shipping:** yes
